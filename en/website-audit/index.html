A website audit that does not end with a list of errors

A useful website audit should do more than list technical issues. It should explain what the evidence means for visitors, trust, enquiries and the next investment in your website.

SIGEL Web Intelligence is a comprehensive website audit for businesses, marketing teams and agencies that need to know not only what is wrong with a website, but above all what should be fixed first. It combines technical evidence, UX, accessibility, offer communication and business interpretation in one prioritised output.

What is a website audit?

A website audit is a systematic analysis of a website. Depending on its scope, it may review technical health, indexation, performance, mobile usability, content, navigation, trust and the website's ability to move a visitor towards an enquiry or purchase.

The problem with many automated audits is that they stop at a list of metrics. A red score does not tell you whether an issue is a major business barrier, a minor technical imperfection or a cosmetic detail that can wait. SIGEL therefore adds an interpretation and prioritisation layer.

What does a comprehensive website audit review?

  • Technical performance: desktop and mobile Lighthouse scores, LCP, FCP, CLS, TBT, Speed Index and Time to Interactive.
  • SEO and indexation: robots.txt, sitemap, metadata, canonical URLs, heading structure, links and core search-engine signals.
  • Mobile UX: viewport behaviour, horizontal overflow, clickable elements, readability and the user journey on smaller screens.
  • Accessibility: contrast, text size and touch-target measurements with concrete findings for manual review.
  • Offer communication: whether visitors quickly understand what the business offers, who it is for, why they should trust it and what to do next.
  • Trust and decision path: calls to action, proof, friction, consistency and the points where a visitor may lose confidence.
  • Brand strategy: brand archetype, customer motivations and a recommended communication direction.

Website audit versus SEO audit

An SEO audit primarily reviews discoverability in search engines: indexation, technical barriers, content structure and signals connected to organic traffic. That matters, but it does not answer the whole question.

A website can have solid SEO and still fail to generate enquiries. It can be fast but unclear. It can attract traffic but fail to build trust. A comprehensive website audit therefore connects SEO with UX, offer clarity, trust and the conversion path. See the detailed comparison in SEO audit vs website audit.

When is a website audit worth doing?

  • the website receives traffic but generates too few enquiries or orders,
  • advertising brings visitors but the results do not match the spend,
  • the business is planning a redesign and does not want to rely on opinions alone,
  • the offer has changed and the current website no longer represents the business,
  • management or an agency needs an external basis for a decision,
  • technical tools show healthy scores but the website still underperforms commercially.
