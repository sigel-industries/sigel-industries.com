01{{ t.home_faq.groups[0][0] }}

Is SIGEL just an SEO or technical audit? +

No. SEO, speed and technical health are only one part. SIGEL works as business intelligence for websites: it connects measured evidence with UX, accessibility, offer clarity, trust, archetypes and a priority roadmap. The result is not another error list, but decision support showing what to fix first.

Is the analysis automated or human-reviewed? +

The base is created by an analytical system: signal collection, technical checks, custom scoring, UX heuristics and interpretation. Every report is reviewed personally before delivery — so the output has clear priorities and makes practical sense.

When does Web Intelligence make the most sense? +

It makes the most sense before investing in advertising, before a redesign, when the website gets too few enquiries, or when the offer is good but visitors do not understand it clearly enough. SIGEL does not add another marketing layer blindly; it first shows where the website may be losing clarity, trust or performance.

02{{ t.home_faq.groups[1][0] }}

What if the website has good technical scores but still does not work commercially? +

That is exactly where SIGEL is useful. Technical health is only one layer. A website can be fast, indexable and formally correct while still failing to explain the offer, build trust, guide visitors to action or speak to the right customer type.

What do brand and customer archetypes mean? +

The brand archetype describes how the company comes across on the website: expert, creator, guide, innovator or another type of brand role. Customer archetypes help define the motivations, doubts and proof different visitor types need in order to understand and trust the offer.

What does the output not guarantee? +

The report does not guarantee revenue growth, better search rankings or advertising campaign results. It is diagnostic decision support: it shows risks, barriers and priorities worth addressing before sending more budget into the website, advertising or redesign.

03{{ t.home_faq.groups[2][0] }}

How much does it cost and who is it for? +

The Web Intelligence report costs €199 per URL. The service is for businesses only and is invoiced. The price covers the complete scope: measured technical and UX signals, offer communication, the archetype playbook, a priority roadmap, evidence data and personal review of every report.

Why pay when Lighthouse and PageSpeed are free? +

Lighthouse and PageSpeed measure the technical layer: speed, accessibility, basic SEO. They do not tell you whether visitors understand the offer, why they do not trust it or which of the many red numbers to fix first. SIGEL uses these measurements as one input and adds UX, communication, trust and a priority roadmap.

What if the report tells me nothing new? +

Look at the sample report first so you know what you get. If the delivered report does not match the described scope, you can file a complaint under the Terms and Conditions.
